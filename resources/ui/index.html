Low End

Bass amp, pedalboard, looper and music tutor.

No licence key is needed for this build.

Presets, sets and scenes
A preset is a whole rig. A set is an ordered list of presets; each is a scene, A to F on the footswitches.
The board
Tap a pedal's switch to turn it on or off; tap the pedal to edit it; hold it to move or remove it. Tap the amp's name to change model.
Knobs
Drag up or down. Double-click to reset; hold or right-click for MIDI Learn.
Learn
Import a song or a video: Low End finds the chords, the key and the beat. Separate it to mute the bass and play along. A and B set a loop.
