LOW END 1.0.0 - TEST BUILD
Amanorsac Studio · amanorsac.studio

WHAT THIS IS
A test build of Low End, a bass amp, pedalboard and looper for the stage. It is
not the released product. It needs the test key you were sent, and it stops
working when that key expires.

INSTALLING
Windows   Run LowEnd-1.0.0-Tester-Windows.exe and follow the installer.
macOS     Open LowEnd-1.0.0-Tester-macOS.pkg and follow the installer.

YOUR TEST KEY
The first time you open Low End it asks for the key. Paste the whole key, which
is one long line of letters and numbers with a full stop in the middle, and
choose Unlock. Until you do there is no sound. The key is remembered, so you
are asked once. The header shows how many days are left. When the key expires
Low End stops until you enter a new one; ask hello@amanorsac.studio.
The key checks itself on your computer. It never connects to the internet.

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
  Standalone  C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\
              /Applications/Low End.app
  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Low End.vst3
              /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Low End.vst3
  Audio Unit  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Low End.component
  Stem separation (optional, about 170 MB)
              C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\Models\
              /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/Low End/Models/
The installer shows these paths and lets you add a second location if you
keep your plug-ins somewhere else.

YOUR PRESETS AND SETTINGS
  Windows   Documents\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\
  macOS     ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/Low End/
Uninstalling does not delete these.

TELLING US WHAT YOU FIND
Write to hello@amanorsac.studio. Say what you did, what you expected and what
happened, and which computer and audio interface you use. Nothing is sent from
Low End for you: it collects nothing and has no usage reporting.

IF YOUR DAW DOES NOT SEE IT
1. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's preferences.
2. Check the folder above is in your DAW's plug-in scan paths.
3. Restart the DAW.
Still missing? hello@amanorsac.studio

UNINSTALLING
  Windows   Settings > Apps > Low End > Uninstall
  macOS     Run Uninstall Low End.command, or delete the files listed above.
