LOW END 1.0.0
Amanorsac Studio · amanorsac.studio

WHAT THIS IS
A bass amp, pedalboard and looper for the stage, with a Learn tab that writes
out the chords and notes of a song and separates it into stems to play along.

INSTALLING
Windows   Run LowEnd-1.0.0-Windows.exe and follow the installer.
macOS     Open LowEnd-1.0.0-macOS.pkg and follow the installer.

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
  Standalone  C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\
              /Applications/Low End.app
  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Low End.vst3
              /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Low End.vst3
  Audio Unit  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Low End.component
  Stem separation (optional, about 170 MB)
              C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\Models\
              /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/Low End/Models/
The installer shows these paths and lets you add a second location if you
keep your plug-ins somewhere else.

YOUR PRESETS AND SETTINGS
  Windows   Documents\Amanorsac Studio\Low End\
  macOS     ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/Low End/
Uninstalling does not delete these.

NO ACTIVATION
There is no key and no account. Amp, pedals, looper, song analysis and stem
separation all run on your computer, and your audio never leaves it. The one
thing that uses the internet is the YouTube browser in the Learn tab, and only
when you open it.

IF YOUR DAW DOES NOT SEE IT
1. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's preferences.
2. Check the folder above is in your DAW's plug-in scan paths.
3. Restart the DAW.
Still missing? hello@amanorsac.studio

UNINSTALLING
  Windows   Settings > Apps > Low End > Uninstall
  macOS     Run Uninstall Low End.command, or delete the files listed above.
